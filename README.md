# 🍪 Interactive Cookie Baking Experience

A delightful web-based interactive narrative that takes users through the cozy process of baking and decorating holiday cookies. Create your perfect cookie design in a warm, festive kitchen atmosphere!

## ✨ Features

### Immersive Environment
- **Cozy Kitchen Scene**: Brown wallpaper, window with red curtains, and gentle snowfall create a warm festive atmosphere
- **Animated Snowfall**: Beautiful snow animation visible throughout the experience
- **Smooth Transitions**: Seamless progression through each baking step

### Interactive Steps

#### 1. **Cookie Cutting** 🍪
Choose from three delightful shapes:
- 🍪 Gingerbread Man
- 🎅 Santa Hat
- ⛄ Snowman

#### 2. **Icing the Cookie** 🎨
Select your icing color:
- ❤️ Red
- 💚 Green
- 🤍 White

#### 3. **Drawing with Icing Pen** ✏️
Free-draw decorations with three icing pen colors:
- Black
- White
- Red

Use your mouse to draw creative patterns directly on your cookie!

#### 4. **Adding Sprinkles** ✨
Drag and drop or click to add sprinkles:
- 🔴 Round sprinkles (red, green, white mix)
- ⭐ Star-shaped sprinkles
- ❤️ Heart-shaped sprinkles

Each sprinkle falls with a delightful bounce animation!

#### 5. **Finale** 🎉
Enjoy your completed cookie masterpiece:
- Cookie displayed on an elegant plate
- Soft Christmas melody plays
- Warm, brightened scene celebrating your creation

## 🎮 How to Use

### Navigation
- **Arrow Buttons**: Left and right arrows on the sides of the screen guide you through steps
- **Step Indicators**: Click the dots at the bottom to jump between steps
- **Progressive Flow**: Each step builds on the previous, creating a natural baking sequence

### Interactions
- **Hover Effects**: Options glow softly when hovered over
- **Click to Select**: Instant visual feedback for shapes, colors, and options
- **Draw**: Press and drag mouse to draw with icing pen
- **Sprinkles**: Click canvas or drag sprinkle options to add decorations

## 🚀 Getting Started

Simply open `index.html` in a modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge).

No installation or build process required!

```bash
# Option 1: Open directly
open index.html

# Option 2: Use a local server (recommended)
python -m http.server 8000
# Then visit http://localhost:8000
```
